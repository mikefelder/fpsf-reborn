Art at FPSF

FPSF is more than a music festival-- with Eleanor Tinsley Park and Allen Parkway as their canvases, it's a chance for Houston and area artists to feature works before tens of thousands of fest goers.

A successful installation creates an ambiance and enhances people's experience in the festival grounds. It should be large enough to make an impact and built to withstand the weather and thousands of attendees. For more info and to submit your idea, click here.
