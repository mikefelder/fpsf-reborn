    PREVIEW BREAKFAST

    This unique breakfast service will be choreographed to original music by Dan the Automator of The Gorillaz and Deltron 3030 while Bun B serves up spoken word, classic UGK lyrics, and newer material. Keeping in timed synchronicity, Chef Speer of Uchi will head the 5 course service with Hell’s Kitchen champion Chef Ja'Nel infusing her signature style in each dish.

    Guests will be treated to novel cocktails by Justin Burrow whose new drinkery is nationally-recognized local favorite Captain Foxheart's Bad News Bar, who will create the cocktails, especially for this event, using a limited-edition vodka, Absolut Texas. 

    Extremely limited seating, May 31, 2014 at 9:30am. Available only to FPSF ticket holders as an upgrade, and can be purchased here.

    FANCY PANTS PASS

     So, Why Fancy Pants?

    A huge element of the festival is the Fancy Pantsexperience! Fancy Pants pass holders will enjoy rapid entry to the event each day, and access to three massive luxury air-conditioned tents, each transformed into a climate-controlled lounge with seating and private bars with shorter lines, as well as a private viewing area.

    Decorating and planning the spaces are Fancy Pants director Dutch Small, along with licensed interior designers Jeannie Wu, Sarah Johnson, Amanda Meininger, and Daphne Yuanidas of Gensler, the world's leading architecture firm, locally known for their design of the new Houston Ballet Center for Dance. Each lounge will be wholly transformed into a different theme, and each will be a total selfie-zone.
