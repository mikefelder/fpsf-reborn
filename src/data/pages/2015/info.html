WHAT

Free Press Summer Festival (FPSF) is an annual music and arts festival held in Houston, Texas.

WHEN

June 6 & 7, 2015, Saturday 11am-11pm, Sunday 11am-10pm. The festival will take place rain or shine, unless otherwise posted. FPSF is for all ages. Re-entry allowed until 5:00 PM as long as your wristband has been scanned on your way out.

WHERE

Eleanor Tinsley Park in Houston, TX. Click here for map. Buy passes online or at the gate

PARKING & TRANSPORTATION

Ride with @Uber_Houston. Be safe, be smart, grab an Uber to & from FPSF. Use the promo code FPSF2015 & get $20 off your first ride! The Teal Lot (near the FPSF entrance) will be the designated @Uber pickup and drop-off area.

RIDE YOUR BIKE. There are plenty of racks and places to lock up in the Teal Lot. DropOff/PickUp + Bicycles @fpsf

PARKING. Eleanor Tinsley Park will charge $12 to park starting at 9am. Park in the Green or Red Lots. The Metro Light Rail runs directly to the festival site as well. As always, we encourage attendees to get to and from the festival as safely as possible. Take the Metro, or find a designated driver. Here is quick list of taxi suggestions we found.

RIDE THE RAIL. The Main Street Metro Rail on will pick you up and drop you off at Eleanor Tinsley Park and it’s only $1.25 each way! The rail runs every 12 minutes each day and is ADA accessible. From Downtown Houston, take the Southbound Redline Metro Rail route to Eleanor Tinsley Park.

Rail hours.
Saturday: Southbound, rail runs until 2:45am. Northbound, rail runs until 1am
Sunday: Southbound, rail runs until 11:25pm. Northbound, rail runs until 12:29am

SAFETY FIRST, KIDS!

Our first aid team and medics are volunteers, employees and/or sponsors of the festival and are on site to assist you, not arrest you. Refusal of medical attention, especially in the houston heat and humidity, can be fatal.

Please make note of the various first aid stations which are staffed by volunteer medics. If you or a friend need medical assistance and are unable to locate or safely walk to a first aid station, raise your hand and ask someone to locate the nearest staff member or volunteer. Everyone is trained to help you promptly find help.

IMPORTANT HEAT EXHAUSTION PREVENTION

Houston is hot and humid during the summer and while everyone should do their best to have a great time, you should also take precautions to prevent heat related illnesses and keep an eye out for others who might be experiencing heat exhaustion or heat stroke. We have a large medical team and first aid stations throughout the festival. Should you or someone else need medical attention, just ask the nearest person to you to find any of our staff members who can assist you. Click here for a PDF containing signs and symptoms of heat related health issues.

Prevent heat exhaustion and heatstroke by wearing light colored, loose fitting clothes; drink plenty of water- if you’re not peeing every few hours, you might be dehydrated; wear sunscreen and a hat; take a break in the shade if you feel like the heat is getting to you.

ADA ACCESS AT FPSF

ADA drop off zone is at Lantern Point and Murworth, right next to the entrance. There will plenty of ADA accessible parking on-site. The main entrance is accessible. Should a line form, you may proceed to the front and ask a gate manager to help expedite your entry.

In order to access the ADA viewing area, we will be issuing ADA wristbands to those that require it at the Box Office. The ADA wristband does not gain the attendee access to the park if it isn't accompanied by another wristband.
