About FPSF


Free Press Summer Festival is a two day celebration of arts and music held in Houston. FPSF 2015 will be located at Eleanor Tinsley Park. FPSF, as it is affectionately referred to by many of its fans, has had such world class artists as Jack White, Calvin Harris, Iggy Pop, Vampire Weekend, Willie Nelson, Snoop Dogg, Flaming Lips, Lauren Hill, and hundreds of others grace it's stage. Held on the periphery of downtown along the sprawling Buffalo Bayou, FPSF's unique blend of cityscape and natural environment create the perfect setting for it's melange of visual and interactive arts, delicious local fare, and 6 stages of music to be enjoyed by it's enthusiastic and diverse crowd. FPSF 2015 will be held June 6th and 7th.
