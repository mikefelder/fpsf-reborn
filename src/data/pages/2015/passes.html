PASS Faqs

  • No individual day passes will be sold.
  • Fpsf is an all ages event.
  • Free entry for children 5 and under with a paid adult entry. Be precautious of heat safety with kids.
  • Passes allow access to both days of the weekend festival.
  • All pass sales are final. However, upgrades are permitted.
  • Attendees are allowed one re-entry per day through the assigned re-entry gate where wristbands must scanned before 5:00 pm in order to get re-entry at a later point in the day. There is no age limit on this.
  • Please note that no lost or stolen passes will be replaced. You must purchase another pass to gain entry.
  • Passes purchased through unauthorized dealers will be considered counterfeit and void.
  • Fpsf reserves the rights to use photos and video captures of fest goers for promotional use without compensation or notice.
