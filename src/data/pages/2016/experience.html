Music. All Day Long.

With more than 55 bands over two days each year, Free Press Summer Festival is a music-lover’s dream. Since its inception in 2009, FPSF has featured a diverse lineup ranging from world-renowned acts to up-and-coming artists.

Best Bites Around.

From classic grilled cheese sandwiches to vegetarian friendly fare and delicious desserts, the food options at Free Press Summer Festival have something for even the pickiest eater. Come have a bite from one of your favorite H-Town food trucks or try some new bites at restaurants that hail from across the region.

More to Explore.

Free Press Summer Festival is more than just a music festival. With on-site activations like cell phone charging stations, photo booths, official festival merch, and art installations by local Houston artists, there’s something for every type of music fan.
