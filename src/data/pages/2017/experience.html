Experience FPSF

Music. All Day Long.

With more than 55 bands over two days each year, FPSF is a music-lover’s dream. Since its inception in 2009, FPSF has featured a diverse lineup ranging from world-renowned acts to up-and-coming artists.

SEE THE LINEUP

Best Bites Around.

From classic grilled cheese sandwiches to vegetarian friendly fare and delicious desserts, the food options at FPSF have something for even the pickiest eater. Come have a bite from one of your favorite H-Town food trucks or try some new bites at restaurants that hail from across the region.

GRAB SOME GRUB

More to Explore.

FPSF is more than just a music festival. With on-site activations like cell phone charging stations, photo booths, official festival merch, and art installations by local Houston artists, there’s something for every type of music fan.

Front Row Views + Exclusive Perks

Take your FPSF weekend to 11 with prime views, luxury indulgences and getaways from the crowd. The Platinum experience lets you roll like a rock star with dedicated front of stage viewing areas, Platinum Lounge and Artist Guest Lounge access — plus all-day perks and exclusive amenities. Or go VIP to FPSF and enjoy all–day comfort with unlimited access to the VIP Lounge with private bars, dedicated festival entrance lanes, air-conditioned restrooms, and more premium benefits.
